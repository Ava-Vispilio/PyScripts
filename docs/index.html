What this is

This is an unofficial parody of Aristocrat's Dragon Link Autumn Moon. Aristocrat did not make it and does not endorse it.

It grew out of the Alberta Tech reel "Vibe Coding is Gambling" and Dave Eddy's YSAP Link stitch of that reel.

Nothing here is real wagering. Credits are pretend, cost nothing, and cannot be cashed out.

A text screen has no cabinet art, no sound, and no linked jackpot. The Major and Grand meters grow only from your own spins in this session.

Rules of this version

Coin 1. Every credit amount below is for this coin and changes when you change the coin.

  • 5 reels, 3 rows, 50 fixed lines. Lines pay left to right, starting on the first reel. The bet is 50 credits: 50 lines times the coin. You start with 50,000 credits.
  • WILD lands on every reel except the first. It stands in for every paying symbol. It does not stand in for MOON or ORB, and ORB never pays on a line.
  • MOON pays anywhere on the screen. Three moons pay 150, four pay 1,000, and five pay 12,500, which is 250 times the bet. Three or more moons also start 6 free games. Three more moons during free games add 6 more.
  • Free spins can land DOOR stacks. Every door on one spin opens onto the same symbol. That symbol can be ORB, but never WILD or MOON.
  • 6 or more ORB start Hold and Spin with 3 respins. Each new orb locks and resets the count to 3. Orbs show credits (50, 100, 150, 250, 400, 750) or a jackpot name. Only one MAJOR can show at a time.
  • MINI pays 1,000. MINOR pays 5,000. MAJOR pays its meter, which starts at 25,000. Filling all 15 cells pays the GRAND meter, which starts at 250,000. Both meters grow a little with every paid spin and reset after they pay.
  • A Major or Grand win pauses the game until you collect it. Spin does nothing during that pause.
  • Hold and Spin started during free games returns to those free games.
  • The coin changes only in the base game.
  • Reel strips, weights, and odds are original to this parody and are not Aristocrat's math.
